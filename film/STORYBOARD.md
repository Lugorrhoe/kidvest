## The five moments that must be exceptional

1. **T1, 0:08**: the data line becoming the farm horizon. The whole thesis in one cut.
2. **S5, 0:21**: "Farmer Jo warns" over the sunny farm with "2008" in the parent tag. Dramatic irony; the audience knows.
3. **S8, 0:43**: "Hold." over a black sky and red chips. The emotional centre.
4. **S9, 0:46 to 0:51**: the reward lands, then the sky clears. Relief.
5. **T3 and S12, 0:58 to 1:09**: storm left, "2008" right, "Children never do." The intellectual payoff for an academic audience.

## Readability rules

- Anything the audience must read stays on screen for at least 2.5 s.
- Product UI text the story depends on is framed by the camera at 1.4x or more, so it measures at least 28 px on the 1080p frame.
- Captions never overlap product text; they sit in the stage margin or over sky.
- No more than one caption and one parent tag on screen at the same time.
